Look Back · 2026 Retro

Your quick-fire retro of 2026

Start with a gut-level look back at your year. 3 wins, 3 losses, 1 surprise. Three AI prompts help you: identify from your gut, cross-reference specific items with real data, and get a full-picture analysis of your whole year.

🤖 Prompt 1 · Help me identify
What really counts as a win or loss?
I'm the community manager for [YOUR GAME]. I need to do a quick retro of 2026, but I'm not sure what really counts as a win, a loss, or a surprise. Ask me guiding questions — one at a time — to help me identify: - 3 wins: moments where the community really moved a KPI, a mood, or a behavior - 3 losses: efforts that didn't land, or things we invested in that didn't pay off - 1 surprise: something that happened I didn't predict Push me to be specific and honest. Distinguish real wins from noise.
🤖 Prompt 2 · Cross-reference with real data
What do the numbers actually show?
I've completed my gut-level retro for [YOUR GAME]: Wins: [PASTE WINS] Losses: [PASTE LOSSES] Surprise: [PASTE SURPRISE] Now I want to verify and enrich it with real data. Here is my 2026 community calendar (events, campaigns, launches): [PASTE CALENDAR LINK OR KEY EVENTS] Here is my community dashboard (sentiment, DAU, retention, engagement): [PASTE DASHBOARD LINK OR KEY METRICS] For each item in my retro, please: 1. Find the matching event or campaign in the calendar and note the dates 2. Check what the dashboard shows for that period — did metrics actually move? 3. Tell me: does the data back my gut? Or did I miss something? 4. Surface patterns I might have missed — moments where the data tells a story I overlooked Help me build a more evidence-backed retro.
🤖 Prompt 3 · Full-picture analysis
Let AI analyze the whole year for me
I need a comprehensive analysis of my 2026 community work for [YOUR GAME]. Community dashboard (Looker): [PASTE LOOKER LINK OR EXPORT] Activities file (all events, campaigns, launches, rituals): [PASTE ACTIVITIES FILE LINK OR DUMP] Analyze it broadly: 1. What trends do you see across the metrics — where did we grow, plateau, or decline? 2. Which activities correlate with meaningful metric movements (positive or negative)? 3. What seasonal or thematic patterns show up across the year? 4. What surprises you in the data that I might miss looking at individual pieces? 5. In one paragraph — what is the business story of my 2026 community? 6. Given all this — what patterns and takeaways should I capture for my retro? Be direct and analytical. Surface what the data actually says — not what I might want to hear.
* Heads-up on permissions: For this to work, you'll need to grant Looker access to Claude/ChatGPT, and you'll need Editor permissions on the dashboard/file on your side to share it with them.

Make the Bet · Strategy Draft

Draft your community strategy — the bet you'll make

One clear bet, rooted in your retro. Use the two prompts below. The first helps you explore your situation — AI asks you questions about your game and audience. The second helps you draft 2–3 concrete strategy options to choose from.

🤖 Prompt 1 · Explore
Understand my situation first
I'm the community manager for [YOUR GAME]. Before I draft a strategy, help me think through my situation. Ask me sharp questions — one at a time — about: - The main business goal for the game this year - Who my most valuable community members are - Where community is currently underdelivering - What competitors or similar games are doing well My 2026 retro insights: [PASTE INSIGHTS FROM EXERCISE 1] Push me to be specific. I'll answer, then we'll draft.
🤖 Prompt 2 · Draft
Draft strategy options
Based on what we discussed about my game and audience, help me draft 2–3 possible community strategies. Each strategy should: - Sound like a stance ("Our community exists to..." or "We bet on...") - Make a clear choice about focus - Connect to a business driver (retention, LTV, virality, brand) - Be sharp enough that it couldn't apply to just any game For each, add 1–2 sentences on why this bet makes sense given my situation.
